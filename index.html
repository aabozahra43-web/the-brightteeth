Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you accept dental insurance?
Yes, we work with most major insurance providers in Egypt. Bring your policy details to your first visit and our front desk will confirm your coverage.
Is teeth whitening safe for enamel?
Our in-clinic whitening uses a controlled, dentist-supervised concentration that's been clinically tested to protect enamel while lifting stains safely.
How long do dental implants last?
With proper care, our titanium implants typically last 15–25 years or longer, and we provide a full aftercare plan to help maximize their lifespan.
Do you treat dental emergencies?
Yes — our emergency line is staffed 24/7 for severe pain, trauma, or swelling. Call the number in the top bar and we'll guide you through next steps.
Can I get a payment plan for treatment?
Absolutely. We offer 0% interest installment plans on implants, orthodontics, and full smile makeovers — ask our coordinator for details.

Severe pain, trauma, or swelling — call now, don't wait.
